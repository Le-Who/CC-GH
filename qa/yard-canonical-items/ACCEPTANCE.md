3. Reload the actual page and re-enter New Yard. Check the committed T2 at its saved world position and matching inventory. Mode choice itself is intentionally not restored.
4. Move by pointer, then cancel a second move. 5. Pick up the item. Verify the record disappears only from the authoritative supported snapshot, inventory returns by exactly one, and replay grants no second copy. New mode admits no food, visitor, wear, gift, photo or generic old-Yard mutation.
6. Place the second real owned T2 and inspect a selected committed slot through the visible Items action. Check approach/leaf focus follows its saved position and avoids the other committed item.
